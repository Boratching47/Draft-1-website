Our Treatments

Precise care,
visible results.

Every treatment at Lumis begins with understanding. No two skins are the same — and neither are two protocols.

Lumis Signature Facial
Our 90-minute cornerstone treatment. Enzyme exfoliation, peptide infusion, manual lymphatic drainage, and gua sha massage. Leaves the skin luminous, plumped, and deeply nourished.
90 min
02
Resurfacing Peel
Medical-grade AHA/BHA and TCA blends calibrated to your Fitzpatrick type. Targets uneven texture, pigmentation, and early lines.
45–60 min
03
LED Photorejuvenation
NASA-derived red and near-infrared light stimulates fibroblast activity and ATP production. Clinically validated for collagen synthesis and cellular renewal. Zero downtime.
30–45 min
04
Microneedling
Controlled micro-injury activates the skin's innate repair cascade — stimulating collagen and elastin production. Effective for scarring, laxity, and textural irregularity.
60–75 min
05
Vitamin Infusion
Concentrated vitamin C, B3, and peptide complexes delivered trans-dermally via iontophoresis. Immediate brightness with cumulative structural benefit over a course of sessions.
45 min
06
Hydration Therapy
Hyaluronic acid in multiple molecular weights, locked in with occlusive botanical balms. For compromised, sensitised, or chronically dehydrated skin requiring immediate barrier support.
60 min
07
Bespoke Protocol
For clients requiring a fully individualised approach — combining modalities across multiple visits. Designed following a detailed skin analysis and lifestyle consultation.
