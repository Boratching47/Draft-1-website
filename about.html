About BMBD

Your business
partner,
not just a service.

Business Management by Design was created to give small and medium-sized businesses access to experienced management support without the need to hire another full-time senior manager.

Kelly Bush is an experienced business advisor, entrepreneur and operational manager with more than 20 years of hands-on business management and ownership experience. Over her career, Kelly has co-founded and built more than 30 businesses and supported over 60 businesses across a wide range of industries. That experience has given her a practical understanding of what owners actually face, from building a business in its early stages through to managing people, cashflow, systems, growth, compliance and significant organisational change.

Kelly is practical, down to earth and enjoys working closely with business owners to untangle problems and make things feel more manageable. She believes good advice should be clear, useful and realistic, not buried in jargon or theory.
