About Lumis

Skincare built
on truth,
not trends.

Lumis was founded in 2017 on a single conviction: that the skin deserves honesty. Honest ingredients, honest science, and honest conversations about what will — and won't — work for you.

What We Stand For
Precision

Every ingredient earns its place. Every protocol is measured. We don't guess — we analyse, formulate, and verify.

Transparency

We tell you what we're using, why we're using it, and what you can realistically expect. No false promises, no filler.

Continuity

One practitioner, ongoing relationship. Your skin history travels with you through every visit. Context matters.

Ethics

Cruelty-free, sustainably sourced, independently tested. Our values extend beyond the treatment room.
